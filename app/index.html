    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Five stages, in order. Story trophies cannot be missed; everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.

    ⚠ The one missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. One meditation spot is only available in Chapter 1, and there is no mission replay, so do it before finishing Chapter 1. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Sources: GTA Vibes · Output Lag
