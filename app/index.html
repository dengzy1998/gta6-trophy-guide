    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Five stages, in order. Story first (60–80 hours), then the stranger strands, then activities and counts, then crime, then cleanup and 100% completion. Only one trophy can be missed — see the warning above — and everything else can be finished after the story in free roam.

    Missable trophies

    Positive Mental Attitude is the only potentially missable trophy: meditate at every spot across Leonida, and do it before you finish Chapter 1, because one spot is only open in Chapter 1 and there is no mission replay. Sources: GTA Vibes, Output Lag.

    Story trophies (chapters 1–4, the final mission and the mission trophies) cannot be missed — they unlock by playing the story. Everything else can be done after the story in free roam.
