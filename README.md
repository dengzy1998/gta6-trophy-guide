An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: checklist with saved progress, export/import, filters, a spoiler setting, a roadmap to the Platinum, and a cited guide for every trophy. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## What's inside

- **Checklist of all 55 trophies** — official names, descriptions and grades exactly as PlayStation publishes them (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PlayStation; all single-player, no online trophies). Tick a trophy to earn it; progress is saved in your browser.
- **A guide for every trophy, in our own words, with its sources cited** — what to do, where and how, and when it can be missed. A specific location, step or number is only given where a cited source states it. Sources: Exophase, Push Square, GTA Vibes, Playday.one, Output Lag and KeenGamer.
- **Roadmap to the Platinum** — five stages in order: story (chapters 1–4 + final mission, 60–80 hours), stranger & side-mission strands, activities & counts, crime & robberies, then cleanup and 100% completion.
- **The one missable trophy, flagged everywhere** — Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only trophy that can be missed: one meditation spot is only available in Chapter 1 and there is no mission replay. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free roam.
- **Filters and search** — by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, crime, hidden), by grade, by earned/unearned, plus text search.
- **Spoiler setting (on by default)** — hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them.
- **Export and import** — download your progress as JSON and import it back; it round-trips.
- **Fully offline** — static files under `app/`, no network requests at run time, no Rockstar logo or official artwork.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
(open http://localhost:8080 — or open app/index.html directly; it works offline)
